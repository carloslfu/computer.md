# Acme Marketing Operations Computer

The marketing team's brain. Campaign tracking, content calendar, paid
ads reporting, attribution, website analytics, demand-gen ops.

## Owner

Priya Iyer, Head of Marketing at Acme. Based in Austin (Central time).
Strategic + creative — wants the data tee'd up so she can decide,
not the decision pre-made.

## Business

Acme sells project management software to mid-market teams. Marketing
mix: content (blog, SEO), paid ads (Google + LinkedIn), events
(2-3/year), webinars (monthly), email nurture, influencer/partnership.

Annual marketing budget $1.2M. Team: 4 (Priya + 2 content + 1 ops).

## Tools

- HubSpot — Marketing Hub (forms, emails, workflows, attribution)
- Google Ads — paid search
- LinkedIn Campaign Manager — paid social
- Google Analytics 4 — website traffic
- Ahrefs — SEO, keyword tracking, competitor monitoring
- Webflow — website CMS
- Notion — content calendar, campaign briefs
- Slack — internal team chat
- Figma — creative assets
- Calendly — event registrations

## Standing rules

- Brand voice: always check against ~/brand/voice.md before drafting copy.
- Customer quotes: never publish without explicit written permission (check ~/wiki/permissions/).
- Paid ads: spend changes > $500/day need my approval.
- Email sends: list size > 5K needs my approval; smaller sends can go after content review.
- Campaign launches: post to #marketing-launches Slack before going live.

## Routines

- ~/systems/morning-marketing/ — 8am CT, summary of campaign performance + traffic
- ~/systems/weekly-attribution/ — Mondays 10am CT, MQL → SQL → opportunity flow
- ~/systems/content-calendar/ — Fridays 2pm CT, next 2 weeks calendar review
- ~/systems/competitor-watch/ — daily, scans Ahrefs + competitor blogs, flags new content

## Out of scope

- Sales follow-up on MQLs (Marcus's computer handles)
- Product copy (PM team owns)
- Internal comms (Sarah's computer)
- Recruiting copy (separate computer, HR-owned)

## Worker preference

Prefer Claude Code for long-form drafting (blog posts, ebook drafts,
landing page copy) and multi-file campaign buildouts. Codex for
quick edits, analytics scripts, HubSpot workflow tweaks.

## Preferences

- Copy: short sentences, active voice, no AI buzzwords (no "delve", "leverage", "seamless").
- Headlines: max 8 words, no questions, no clickbait.
- Always show me 3 options before settling on a headline or subject line.
- Metrics: cite the dashboard URL, never paste raw numbers without context.

## What the manager has learned about this machine

- HubSpot "Marketing-MQL" view excludes employees and partners — use this for real numbers.
- Google Ads account has a $5K/day account-level cap (above that, the agency manager kicks in).
- Webflow auto-publishes on Wednesdays at 9am CT — don't push changes Tuesday night unless they're ready to ship.
- Brand voice doc says: don't use the company name in titles ("Acme makes X" is fine in body, not in H1).
